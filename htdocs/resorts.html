Bliss of Nature Resort, Kanatal

Kanatal, Tehri Garhwal, Uttarakhand (8,500 Feet Altitude)

Book on WhatsApp Enquire Now

Experience Pure Mountain Serenity

Set amidst towering deodar and fragrant pine trees at an altitude of 8,500 feet, Bliss of Nature Resort is designed for discerning travelers who seek peaceful mountain seclusion without compromising on modern luxury.

Wake up to golden sunshine bathing the snow-capped Garhwal Himalayan peaks. Spend your afternoons on private wooden sundecks with piping hot Garhwali ginger tea, take guided walks through the Kaudia forest reserve, and gather around the campfire under a brilliant ceiling of millions of stars.
