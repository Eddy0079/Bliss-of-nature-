Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about reaching Kanatal, climate, and booking with BON Holidays.

Bliss of Nature Resort is located on the Chamba-Mussoorie Highway in Kanatal (8,500 ft). The nearest airport is Jolly Grant Airport Dehradun (approx. 90 km / 3 hours drive). The nearest railhead is Rishikesh or Haridwar. You can also drive from Delhi via Meerut - Rishikesh - Chamba - Kanatal (approx. 310 km / 7-8 hours). We provide sanitized private cab pickups from Dehradun, Rishikesh, and Haridwar.
Kanatal has pleasant weather year-round. Summer (April to June) offers cool temperatures between 15°C to 26°C, perfect for escaping city heat. Monsoon (July to August) turns the forests lush green with romantic misty valleys. Autumn & Winter (October to February) offer clear 180° views of snow-capped peaks, with frequent snowfall in December and January.
